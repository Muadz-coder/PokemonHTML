Competitive Battles

Competitive Pokémon involves strategic team building, understanding Pokémon stats, abilities, movesets, and mastering the metagame to excel in tournaments and online battles.

Competitive Pokémon revolves around team building, as it is the cornerstone of success in competitive play. A well-built team is essential for competitive battles, as it ensures that each Pokémon has a specific role and contributes to the team's overall strategy.
