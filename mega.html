Pokémon Champions offers the competitive format by introducing an option to play single or double battles. Ranks are avalaible every season, which resets once the season ends. Held items are avalaible in the shop section, but some aren't avalaible yet for players to unlock.

Every 22 hours, 10 pokémon are shown for the players to choose from. The game features 'most' of the new mega evolutions in Pokémon Legends ZA. This also includes insanely good competitive pokémon from previous championships that you are able to customized its full potential.
